      Session autosaves

      Zephyr autosaves every change during a session. When you say “Zephyr please end session,” those autosaves move here for seven days, then they are deleted. The draft saved at the end of the session stays in Documents.

      Voice commands

      Everything you say goes into the manuscript unless you start with “Zephyr please.” If you pause after “Zephyr please,” you’ll hear a tone, then say the command.

      SayWhat happens
      (just talk)Whenever Zephyr is listening, everything you say goes into the manuscript, except anything that starts with “Zephyr please.” Say “period,” “comma,” “question mark,” “new paragraph,” “open quote,” “close quote.” After “a” or “the” these stay words, so “a dash of salt” and “the Victorian period” are written as said.
      Zephyr please composeStart writing again after a pause.
      Zephyr please pauseStop adding what you say, for example to talk to someone in the car. Zephyr keeps listening for commands.
      Zephyr please add to manuscriptWhile paused, add everything you’ve said since your last command. Also “Zephyr please add that.” It also brings back words Zephyr mistook for a command, or that you said while it was talking.
      Zephyr please new paragraphStart a new paragraph. In a list, starts the next item; say it twice to end the list. While composing you can also just say “new paragraph.”
      Zephyr please new lineStart a new line in the same paragraph, or the next list item.
      Zephyr please scratch thatRemove the last thing you dictated.
      Zephyr please readRead the whole document. Also: “read last paragraph,” “read last sentence,” “read paragraph three,” “read last two paragraphs.”
      Zephyr please edit …Make a change. “Zephyr please edit cut the second sentence of the last paragraph.” “Zephyr please change ‘cold’ to ‘bitter’ in the opening.” If Zephyr asks which part you mean, just answer.
      Zephyr please feedbackHear feedback and numbered suggestions. Add a focus: “Zephyr please feedback on pacing.”
      Zephyr please apply suggestion 2Make that suggested edit. Also “apply suggestions one and three” or “apply all.”
      Zephyr please explain …Ask how to edit. “Zephyr please explain how to tighten a long opening.” Questions work too: “Zephyr please how do I show this instead of telling it?”
      Zephyr please undoUndo the last change this session. Also “undo all” and “redo.”
      Zephyr please create newSave this document and start a new one that begins with a copy of it. “Create new blank” starts empty.
      Zephyr please rename …Rename the current document. Also “Zephyr please call it …”
      Zephyr please open …Switch documents by name. “Zephyr please list documents” reads the names.
      Formatting
      Zephyr please titleMake the current paragraph the title. With words after it, adds a new title line: “Zephyr please title, The Long Road North.”
      Zephyr please heading oneHeading 1. Also heading two, three and four. Add words to create the heading: “Zephyr please heading two, chapter one.”
      Zephyr please normal textTurn the current heading or list item back into an ordinary paragraph.
      Zephyr please bulleted listMake the current paragraph a bullet, or start a list for your next words. Say “new paragraph” for the next bullet.
      Zephyr please numbered listSame as bulleted list, with numbers.
      Zephyr please end listFinish a list and go back to normal paragraphs.
      Zephyr please bold …Bold part of the text: “Zephyr please bold that,” “Zephyr please bold last sentence,” “Zephyr please bold last three words,” “Zephyr please bold the words cold river.” Also “Zephyr please make that bold.”
      Zephyr please italic …Same targets as bold.
      Zephyr please underline …Same targets as bold.
      Zephyr please bold onEverything you say next is bold until “Zephyr please bold off.” Works for italic and underline too.
      Zephyr please remove bold …Take bold off that, the last sentence, or named words. “Zephyr please remove formatting” clears bold, italic and underline.
      Zephyr please formatting helpHear the formatting commands.
      Other
      Zephyr please word countHear the length.
      Zephyr please repeatHear Zephyr’s last answer again.
      Zephyr please back upSend the current draft to Google Drive right now. Zephyr also backs up on its own every couple of minutes and at the end of each session.
      Zephyr please stopSilence Zephyr or cancel a request in progress. Your writing keeps going.
      Zephyr please end sessionSave the draft, archive this session’s autosaves for one week, and stop listening.

      Edit, feedback, explain and automatic punctuation use Claude. Dictation, formatting and read-back work without it.

      Create one at console.anthropic.com. It stays in this browser and is sent only to Anthropic. A spending limit on the key is a good idea.

      Voice and listening
      Lets you say “Zephyr please stop” in the middle of a read-back. If Zephyr hears itself through the car speakers, turn this off.
      Phones often hear “Zephyr” as “suffer” or “sapphire”. With this on, those count too, but only at the start of what you say and only before a real command. Turn it off if your writing uses those words that way.
      Google Drive backup

      Zephyr sends the working draft to Drive every couple of minutes while you write, and the saved draft at the end of each session. Saved drafts become Google Docs in a Zephyr folder. Session backups go to Zephyr › Session backups and are deleted after seven days.
